EZKids (បង្រៀនកូន)

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026. This text is a draft.

1. Who we are

EZKids (បង្រៀនកូន), "the app", is a learning app that helps children aged 3 to 6 learn Khmer and English. It is made by PersonalLifeInsights.

2. In short

We collect as little as possible. The app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. The only personal detail the app can hold is a first name or nickname that a parent chooses to type for the greeting, and it stays on the device.

3. What is stored on your device

The app stores these things on your phone: the child's name or nickname (optional), stars and the day streak, which lessons, quizzes and games were finished, the language and sound choices, and the parent PIN (optional). This is kept only in the app's private storage on the phone. We do not receive it and we cannot see it.

If backup is switched on for your phone (for example Google backup), the phone may copy the app's data to your own Google account. That is controlled by your phone's settings.

4. What uses the internet

To keep lessons up to date, the app downloads lesson content (letters, numbers, colors, shapes and animals) from Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). The app only reads lesson content. It does not send learning progress, names or any other personal information.

As with any internet connection, Google's servers receive technical details such as the IP address when the app connects. The app also works without internet, using the content built into it.

5. Sound and speech

Words are played from recordings inside the app or spoken by your phone's text-to-speech engine. The app does not record sound from the microphone.

6. Children's privacy

The app is designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The parent area can be protected with an optional PIN. Because nothing is collected, nothing is shared, sold or shown to the public. The app has no advertising and no third-party tracking.

7. Your choices

A parent can change or clear the name and turn off the PIN in the parent area. To delete everything the app has stored, clear the app's storage in your phone's settings or uninstall the app.

8. Changes to this policy

If we add new features (such as advertising, accounts or syncing between devices) we will update this policy before they are released. The date of the latest update is shown above.

9. Contact

Contact: personallifeinsights@gmail.com
